# Hevelius constellation engravings (Sky Explorer 0.9.4)

Johannes Hevelius, *Firmamentum Sobiescianum sive Uranographia*, published in *Prodromus Astronomiae* (Gdańsk, 1690). Hevelius died in 1687, so the engravings are in the public domain worldwide (PD-old). Each scan below is marked Public domain on Wikimedia Commons.

Processing for Sky Explorer: mirrored from Hevelius' globe view to the view from Earth where needed (the star fit decides this), paper removed into alpha, rendered as silver lines with a glow, cropped to the IAU boundary, at most 1024 px, aligned to real stars by fitting catalogue stars to the engraved star symbols.
